What To Look For

The case studies show implementation judgement, not generic feature lists.

They cover how transactions move, where systems can fail, how providers are wrapped, how wallets and commissions are reconciled, and how admin teams inspect the work.

Architecture

Service boundaries and orchestration

Provider APIs are handled through dedicated workflows with validation, retries, callbacks, and state tracking.

Reliability

Transaction safety

Wallet flows, audit trails, idempotency patterns, and status checks reduce operational ambiguity.

Operations

Admin and MIS visibility

Reports, receipts, search, dashboards, and service-specific status screens help support teams resolve issues quickly.
