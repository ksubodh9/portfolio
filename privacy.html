Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled on the Subodh Kumar portfolio website.

Plain-English Summary

The portfolio is a project showcase, not a data collection product.

The site links to resumes, GitHub, LinkedIn, Streamlit apps, and case studies. Any information you send through external platforms is handled by those platforms and their policies.

Back to Portfolio Terms
1. Information collected

Basic website use

The site does not intentionally collect personal data beyond normal web server logs. If you contact me through LinkedIn, GitHub, or another external service, that service may process your information.

2. How information is used

Communication only

Information you choose to share is used to respond to your message, discuss opportunities, or continue a professional conversation.

3. Third-party links

External platforms

The site includes links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Streamlit, and downloadable resume files. Those external sites may have their own privacy practices.

4. Security

Reasonable care

No internet transmission is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information through public contact channels.
