Engineering Philosophy

How I think about building AI.

01

Production over prototype

An AI system that only works in a notebook isn't finished. I build for deployment from day one — auth, fallbacks, rate limits, monitoring, real constraints.

”
02

Explainable & grounded

Models earn trust when people can see why. SHAP for ML, citations for RAG, confident refusal when the answer isn't known. No black boxes, no hallucinated facts.

”
03

Always be learning

The field moves weekly. I treat every project as a chance to go deeper — and I bring backend rigour to AI so the new tools stand on a solid foundation.

”
